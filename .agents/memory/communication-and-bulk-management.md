---
name: Communication and bulk management
description: Owner control of inquiry methods, independent post-registration contact and all-opportunity editing.
---

The owner must be able to activate one, two or all inquiry methods (WhatsApp, email and Telegram), and customize their destinations and the inquiry button/message texts.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested choosing any combination rather than one exclusive method.

The owner's contact after participant registration is independently configurable. Do not redirect to a stale form-opening snapshot or hardcoded fallback number; retrieve current settings after a successful registration. Failure to load contact settings must not erase success or prompt a duplicate registration.

**Why:** the user reported that registration forwarded participants to an old number and asked to control the destination.

Keep individual opportunity editing alongside bulk editing of selected properties/content across all opportunities.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested both scopes, not replacement of individual editing.

**How to apply:** owner-only controls; bulk updates are opt-in by property, require confirmation for broad scopes, leave unselected properties unchanged and use an all-or-nothing transaction with the existing seat-allocation rules.
